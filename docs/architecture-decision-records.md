# Architecture Decision Records

This directory contains Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) for significant technical decisions made during the development of this platform.

## Index

| ADR | Title | Status |
|-----|-------|--------|
| ADR-001 | Use Asynchronous Message Bus for Pipeline Communication | Accepted |

## What is an ADR?

An Architecture Decision Record captures a significant design decision, including the context, the decision itself, the consequences, and alternatives considered. ADRs provide a historical record of why the architecture is the way it is.
