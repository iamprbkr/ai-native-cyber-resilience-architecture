# Policy Framework

## Policy Hierarchy

```
Enterprise Security Policy
├── Access Control Policy
│   ├── Zero Trust Access Standard
│   ├── Multi-Factor Authentication Standard
│   └── Privileged Access Management Procedure
├── Incident Response Policy
│   ├── Incident Classification Standard
│   ├── Playbook Development Standard
│   └── Post-Incident Review Procedure
├── Data Protection Policy
│   ├── Data Classification Standard
│   ├── Data Retention Standard
│   └── Data Encryption Procedure
├── AI Governance Policy
│   ├── AI Model Validation Standard
│   ├── Human-in-the-Loop Procedure
│   └── AI Ethics Guidelines
├── Third-Party Risk Policy
│   ├── Vendor Assessment Standard
│   └── Supply Chain Security Standard
└── Compliance Policy
    ├── NIST CSF Alignment Standard
    ├── ISO 27001 Alignment Standard
    └── Audit and Reporting Procedure
```

## Key Policy Principles

1. **Zero Trust** — No implicit trust; verify every request regardless of origin
2. **Least Privilege** — Minimum access required for function, periodically reviewed
3. **Assume Breach** — Design for failure; contain blast radius
4. **Defense in Depth** — Multiple overlapping controls at every layer
5. **Continuous Validation** — Always verify, never trust based on location or network
6. **Human Oversight** — Critical decisions require human approval
7. **Audit Everything** — Immutable audit trail for all security-relevant actions

## Policy Lifecycle

- **Create**: Policy drafted by security team, reviewed by legal/compliance
- **Approve**: Signed off by CISO/executive leadership
- **Publish**: Distributed to all stakeholders, training provided
- **Review**: Annual review minimum, ad-hoc for major changes
- **Retire**: Formal deprecation with replacement policy
