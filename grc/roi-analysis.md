## Non-Financial Benefits

- **Improved resilience posture**: Measurable resilience score improvement
- **Regulatory compliance**: Automated NIST CSF and ISO 27001 mapping
- **Analyst satisfaction**: Reduced alert fatigue, higher-value work
- **Board confidence**: Quantifiable risk reduction metrics
- **Scalability**: Platform scales without proportional headcount increase

## Assumptions

- Based on mid-size enterprise (500–2000 employees)
- SOC team of 5–8 analysts before automation
- Average breach cost: $4.45M per IBM Cost of Data Breach 2025
- AI/LLM costs based on GPT-4o-mini pricing at time of analysis
- Excludes one-time development cost of $50k–$100k for initial deployment
